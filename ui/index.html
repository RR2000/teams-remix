      🐾 Housemates

      They live in your Teams, with any theme (the original too). Each one has its thing: a bowl, a hay bale for the bull, a pumpkin for the ghost, a box of cables for the robot, a parcel for the truck, milk for the Dude.

      Preview

      The preview works like Teams: click and drag the bowls, click the housemates to cuddle them.

      Nicknames

      Type the name as you see it in Teams and the nickname to see instead: chats, lists, notifications. Only you see it, on this PC: nothing changes for the others, and what you write in messages stays as it is. Write Surname Name, without a comma: "Green Luna" changes both "Green, Luna" (colleagues) and "Luna Green" (guests).

      📷 With a photo, that person has your photo instead of theirs in Teams (only you see it too).

      🔎 You can also search by nickname: type it in Teams' search at the top and "Nicknames" shows up above the results (at most 2 people). Click one and Teams opens that person.

      Language

      Start

      At sign-in the app starts by itself in the background and waits for Teams.

      Animations

      Off: the effects always run, because you pick them theme by theme.

      Calls

      When you join a call or a meeting, theme, housemates and nicknames disappear by themselves and come back when the call ends. Teams doesn't reopen, so the call doesn't drop. Otherwise, at any time just switch off "On in Teams" at the top.

      Updates

      Teams

      To colour Teams, the app reopens it with a control channel that only works on this PC. While that channel is open, other programs on the PC could read what's in Teams. This button closes it and puts Teams back as it is.

      App

      Teams Remix stays on in the background to put the theme, housemates and nicknames back when Teams reloads.
